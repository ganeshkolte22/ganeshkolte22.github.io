About

Infrastructure consulting with a delivery focus

Roughly fourteen years of enterprise infrastructure experience, now concentrated on application delivery, cloud-native platforms and security.

I work as a Senior Consultant at BRAVECX, an F5 partner, in professional services and pre-sales with a focus on the NGINX Plus ecosystem. My engagements usually start with discovery and architecture reviews, then move through POCs, Statements of Work and implementation.

Most of my customers are large, regulated enterprises, particularly in banking, financial services and insurance, where security, auditability and uptime are not optional. I enjoy translating those requirements into practical designs that operations teams can actually run.

Alongside client work I keep my hands on the tooling: Kubernetes clusters, Helm charts, Azure networking and Linux high-availability setups.
